Upload Product

Add products to your Shop catalogue and optionally assign them to homepage sections and Shop collections.

Every product must have an actual product category. Homepage placements are optional and can be multiple. Additional Shop collections are also optional and can be multiple. Each homepage product placement has a maximum of 10 products. The general Shop catalogue has no fixed product limit.
Select the homepage product sections where this product should appear. You may select multiple sections.
These collections are separate from homepage product placements. A product can belong to multiple collections.
Select up to four images from your device. The first image will be the main product image.
Existing image URL or path (legacy products) Use this only when working with an existing externally hosted or locally referenced image. New product uploads should use image files.

Website Image Management

Manage the website logo, hero slides, circular category images and additional Shop collection slides.

Website images are managed through the backend image-storage system so that updated images can be displayed to visitors across different devices. The collection slide images are: Trending, Featured, Recommended, Must Have and Explore. Replacing a collection slide image does not change the products assigned to that collection.
